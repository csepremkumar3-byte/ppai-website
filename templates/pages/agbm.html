Annual General Body Meeting

Annual General Body Meeting Procedures

  1. The date of the annual general body meeting shall be announced by the council at least four weeks before the meeting and all notices of motions shall be received by the general secretary by the end of October. The out-going President will address the meeting. In case the president is unable to attend the annual general meeting the presidential address will be sent and read at the meeting.
  2. The venue of the meetings shall be decided by the executive council.
  3. The agenda and resolutions for the annual general meeting shall be circulated to the members before the date of the meeting.
  4. The reading of technical papers will be fully encouraged at all meetings of the association. Discussions, purely of plant protection interest could however be held at the annual general meetings.

Withdrawal or Removal of Members

  1. A member may withdraw from the association by signifying his/her desire to do in a letter addressed to the secretary. The association shall not be liable to return any fee that may have been paid by the member in advance.
  2. A member who has withdrawn and whose subscriptions is not in arrears shall automatically recover the privileges of membership without reelection, if he joins the association within a year of withdrawn and he shall be liable to pay all dues as if he had not withdrawn at all.
  3. The general body shall have the power to remove any member of the association from its rolls.

Amendment of the Rules

  1. No amendment of alteration shall be made in purpose of the Association unless it is voted by 2/3rd members present at a meeting convened for the purpose and confirmed by 2/3rd of the members present at a second special meeting.
  2. Proposals for an amendment of these rules may be made either by the council or any member duly seconded. In the latter, case, it must reach the secretary in writing in time for circulation to all members of the association at least 1 month before extraordinary meeting by the end of October for the annual meeting.
  3. The executive council may frame or alter bye-laws for efficient working of the association not inconsistent with its rules.

Winding Up

In case, the society/association had to be woundup, the property and funds of the society/association that remain after full satisfaction of the liabilities of the society/association will be transferred or paid to some other institution with similar aims and objectives.
