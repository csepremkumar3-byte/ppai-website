Other Publications

Books & Proceedings of Conferences

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) has released several important publications during its national and international group events. Advances made in the field of plant protection have been compiled and published for the benefit of students, researchers, state agriculture department personnel, and farmers.

  1. Indian Journal of Plant Protection
    50 Volumes published till 2022 (Quarterly research journal established in 1973).
  2. Plant Health Management for Food Security – Issues and Approaches (2016)
    Plant Protection Association of India, NBPGR Hyderabad & Astraa Publications. Eds. Gururaj Katti, K. Anitha, G.S. Laha, N. Somasekhar, B. Sharat Babu and K.S. Varaprasad (Eds.). (Proceedings of the International Conference on Plant Health Management for Food Security 2012).
  3. Extended Summaries of the National Conference on Organic Waste Utilization and Eco-Friendly Technologies for Crop Protection (2007)
    Published by Plant Protection Association of India, Hyderabad.
  4. Resource Management in Plant Protection During Twenty First Century (2002)
    Proceedings of the National Conference in 2002 (Published in Two Volumes).
  5. Forty Years of Plant Protection in India
    Commemorative Special Publication by Plant Protection Association of India.
  6. Plant Protection Towards Sustainability (1997)
    Proceedings of the National Symposium, 1997 — Special Issue of the Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP).
  7. Plant Protection and Environment (1995)
    Proceedings of the National Symposium on Integrated Pest Management and Environment, 1995.
  8. Pheromones in Integrated Pest Management (1994)
    Proceedings of the National Workshop on Use of Pheromones in IPM, 1994.
  9. Pests and Pest Management – Changing Scenario (1992)
    Proceedings of the National Seminar, 1992.
  10. Plant Protection in Field Crops (1986)
    Proceedings of the National Seminar on Plant Protection in Field Crops, 1986.
