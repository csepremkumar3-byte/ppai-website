Awards Nomination Guidelines

Who may be nominated Members of the Association and scientists working in the field of plant protection, as per the eligibility prescribed for each award.
Who may nominate Members of the Association; proposals may also be submitted by the parent institution of the nominee.
Nomination to be addressed to The General Secretary, Plant Protection Association of India, ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad – 500 030.
Documents to be enclosed Completed nomination proforma, bio-data of the nominee, list of publications and a statement of the specific contribution for which the award is claimed.
Call for nominations Notified on the Awards page of the official portal, www.ppaiorg.in, along with the closing date for the year.
Selection Nominations are scrutinised and the awardees are decided by the Executive Council; awards are presented at the Annual General Body Meeting or at a conference of the Association.
