Books & Special Publications

Plant Health Management for Food Security: Issues and Approaches (2016)

Published by the Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad, this comprehensive scientific volume addresses pressing national and global plant health challenges essential for safeguarding food security.

The publication compiles peer-reviewed treatises on climate change impacts on pest dynamics, biosecurity protocols, transboundary pest incursions, and next-generation integrated pest management paradigms in Indian agriculture.

  • Scientific Scope: Evaluates sustainable crop protection strategies, ecological pest monitoring, bio-risk mitigation, and regulatory plant quarantine frameworks.
  • National Impact: Serves as an authoritative technical reference for agricultural scientists, policy planners, extension specialists, and research scholars across ICAR institutes and State Agricultural Universities.
  • Publisher: Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad.

Resources Management in Plant Protection (Volumes I & II) (2002)

Released as a landmark two-volume reference set, this major publication synthesizes research findings and practical methodologies for managing biological, botanical, chemical, and cultural resources in agricultural crop protection.

The set provides a holistic framework for sustainable resource utilization, minimizing chemical pesticide reliance, and preserving natural biodiversity in agro-ecosystems across diverse Indian cropping regions.

  • Volume I: Focuses on biological control agents, microbial biopesticides, botanical formulations, host plant resistance, and cultural management strategies.
  • Volume II: Focuses on chemical management strategies, pesticide resistance monitoring, novel insecticide chemistries, advanced spray application technology, and environmental toxicology.
  • Publisher: Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad.

Pests and Pest Management in India — The Changing Scenario (1992)

This foundational volume provides a detailed historical synthesis analyzing long-term shifts in insect pest dynamics, vector-borne plant diseases, nematode infestations, and weed ecological shifts following the Green Revolution in India.

The book documents historical pest outbreaks, pesticide usage patterns, resistance developments, and policy evolution, serving as an invaluable baseline for comparative epidemiological and entomological research.

  • Historical Analysis: Comprehensive retrospective on five decades of pest status shifts across cereal, pulse, oilseed, cotton, and horticultural cropping systems.
  • Policy Guidance: Outlines foundational guidelines for pesticide regulation, biological control integration, and national plant protection policy frameworks.
  • Publisher: Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad.
