About Us

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) is an autonomous scientific, non-profit society established in 1972, dedicated to advancing plant health, sustainable crop biosecurity, and agricultural science throughout India.

(Regn. No. S399 of 1949-50 under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860) • National Headquarters: ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad

Genesis & Founding Leadership

The Plant Protection Association of India was founded on 4th December 1972 at the Central Plant Protection Training Institute (CPPTI), Rajendranagar, Hyderabad. Registered under the Andhra Pradesh Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Registration No. S399), the society was conceived by Founder President Dr. K. K. Nirula alongside distinguished plant protection specialists to address pressing national challenges in crop health and pest management.

With its permanent national headquarters located at the ICAR-NBPGR Regional Station, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad, the Association coordinates scientific consultations, national policy recommendations, and research initiatives with central and state agricultural institutions.

Over 50 Years of National Scientific Service

PPAI celebrated its landmark Golden Jubilee (1972–2022), commemorating fifty years of continuous contributions to Indian agricultural science. Over the decades, the Association has evolved into a multidisciplinary body encompassing Agricultural Entomology, Plant Pathology, Nematology, Weed Science, Plant Biosecurity, and Pesticide Chemistry.

Today, the Association represents a national community of over 1,900 life member scientists, faculty members, research scholars, and extension professionals across ICAR institutes, State Agricultural Universities, and partner agricultural bodies nationwide.

Indian Journal of Plant Protection

The Association publishes the double-blind peer-reviewed Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) quarterly in March, June, September, and December (Print ISSN: 0253-4355). Digitally hosted on the official ICAR ePubs portal, IJPP publishes original research articles, comprehensive scientific reviews, and short communications on applied plant protection and integrated pest management.

The journal is indexed in leading national and international bibliographic databases, including the UGC-CARE List, CABI Abstracts, Indian Citation Index (ICI), Chemical Abstracts, and FAO Agrindex.

Core Mandate & Objectives

The activities of the Plant Protection Association of India are directed toward achieving key agricultural and scientific objectives:

  • Integrated Pest Management: Promoting sustainable, bio-intensive pest and disease management technologies that safeguard environmental and human health.
  • Scientific Dissemination: Publishing quarterly issues of IJPP, specialized symposia proceedings, and technical monographs.
  • Conferences & Symposia: Organizing biennial national conferences, seminars, and consultative workshops on emerging plant protection issues.
  • Recognition & Fellowships: Honoring distinguished scientists with Fellow of PPAI (FPPAI) recognition, honorary fellowships, and lifetime achievement awards.
  • Academic Mentorship: Sponsoring annual awards for the Best M.Sc. and Ph.D. Theses in plant protection disciplines to encourage young agricultural researchers.
