About us

History

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) is a scientific society established in 1972. It is non-profit organization, registered under the Andhra Pradesh (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F.) on 4th December, 1972. Its membership is open to all those scientists interested in promotion of Plant Protection and is dedicated for that cause.

As per the 1972 memorandum, it was decided that the permanent headquarters of PPAI would be Hyderabad. The Association started functioning from the location of Central Plant Protection Training Institute and set up its office in the premises of the institute. In 1998, the office set up was shifted to ICAR – National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources and since then has been continuing to operate from this institute till this day. In 2017, PPAI was permitted to construct its own building in an area located under the jurisdiction of Professor Jayashankar Telangana State Agricultural University, Hyderabad. All the set ups of the Association will be gradually shifted to the new building and PPAI new premises will be fully operational by January 2025.

Aims and Objectives of the Association

Since inception, the aims and objectives mentioned in PPAI memorandum have been based on a set of core underlying aspirations of advancing the cause of plant protection research and development, disseminate plant protection knowledge and maintain a consistent link among the plant protection workers belonging to diverse organizations in the country. These objectives have undergone revision and modifications to align with advances in research and development, technologies as well as infrastructure growth in areas related to plant protection. Following are the existing objectives of the Association:

  • To advance the cause of plant protection by encouraging and promoting plant protection research and education in the country.
  • To provide a forum for dissemination of information related to plant protection through a journal.
  • To bring about exchange of views and ideas on plant protection by organising lectures, discussion, seminars, symposia etc.
  • To maintain a link between plant protection workers in India.
  • To involve in any such activity which will be useful for the advancement and adoption of plant protection techniques developed by the scientific community.

Executive Council of PPAI

  1. There is an executive council responsible to carry on all affairs of the association.
  2. The executive council consists of Honorary Patron, the President; Vice President; General Secretary; Assistant Secretary; Treasurer and Eight councillors. All except the Honorary Patron (Plant Protection Adviser to the Government of India) will be elected by the General body. All the office bearers are elected for a period of two years.
  3. The general secretary is responsible for recording the proceedings of the meetings of the association and of the council and for conducting day to day business of the association. The assistant secretary assists the secretary in all matters connected with the association, and shall be in charge in the absence of the secretary. The treasurer maintains the account, receive deposits and disburses money on behalf of the association according to directions of the council.
  4. The funds are spent only to the attainment of the objects of the society and no portion thereof shall be paid or transferred directly or indirectly to any of its members through any means.
  5. The funds of the association are in the name of the Plant Protection Association of India operated jointly by General Secretary and Treasurer and the latter shall make payments endorsed by the secretary in respect of bills not exceeding ₹100/- at a time, Bills for sums in excess of this amount shall be jointly endorsed by the secretary and the president to the extent of ₹1000/- before they will be valid for payment by the treasurer.
  6. There is a reserve fund of the association governed as follows:
    • There is a reserve fund of the association.
    • The reserve fund is not used on recurring items.
    • All the admission fees and 50% of life membership fee received form the reserve fund.
    • The executive council by decision of the general body by two thirds majority, can sanction any expenditure out of the reserve fund but not exceeding 50%.
  7. All accounts of the association are audited annually by an accountant nominated by the executive council. The treasurer submits to the accountant by the end of April each year the accounts of the association for the previous year for the purpose of auditing. The auditor’s statement of accounts are circulated to all members before the annual general body meeting.

Meeting of the Council

  • Council meetings are held periodically at the discretion of the president or at the request of 1/3 of the members of council.
  • The date and time of holding each meeting of the council are notified by the secretary to each member of the council at least 2 weeks before the date on which such meetings are to be held. Non-receipt of such notice by any member of the council in no way invalidate the proceedings of the meeting. One-third of the members of the executive council form the quorum.
  • Extraordinary meetings of the council are called by the president on a requisition signed by the members of the council.

Meetings of the Association

  1. Ordinary meetings of the association are presided over by the President or in his absence by one of the Vice-Presidents or by one of the members of the council present or by member elected at the meetings.
  2. An extraordinary meeting of the association is called if one third of the ordinary members of the association send a signed requisition to the general secretary for such meeting.

Annual General Body Meeting

  1. The date of the annual general body meeting shall be announced by the council at least four weeks before the meeting and all notices of motions shall be received by the general secretary by the end of October. The out-going President will address the meeting. In case the president is unable to attend the annual general meeting the presidential address will be sent and read at the meeting.
  2. The venue of the meetings shall be decided by the executive council.
  3. The agenda and resolutions for the annual general meeting shall be circulated to the members before the date of the meeting.
  4. The reading of technical papers will be fully encouraged at all meetings of the association. Discussions, purely of plant protection interest could however be held at the annual general meetings.

Withdrawal or Removal of Members

  1. A member may withdraw from the association by signifying his/her desire to do in a letter addressed to the secretary. The association shall not be liable to return any fee that may have been paid by the member in advance.
  2. A member who has withdrawn and whose subscriptions is not in arrears shall automatically recover the privileges of membership without reelection, if he joins the association within a year of withdrawn and he shall be liable to pay all dues as if he had not withdrawn at all.
  3. The general body shall have the power to remove any member of the association from its rolls.

Amendment of the Rules

  1. No amendment of alteration shall be made in purpose of the Association unless it is voted by 2/3rd members present at a meeting convened for the purpose and confirmed by 2/3rd of the members present at a second special meeting.
  2. Proposals for an amendment of these rules may be made either by the council or any member duly seconded. In the latter, case, it must reach the secretary in writing in time for circulation to all members of the association at least 1 month before extraordinary meeting by the end of October for the annual meeting.
  3. The executive council may frame or alter bye-laws for efficient working of the association not inconsistent with its rules.

Winding Up

In case, the society/association had to be woundup, the property and funds of the society/association that remain after full satisfaction of the liabilities of the society/association will be transferred or paid to some other institution with similar aims and objectives.
