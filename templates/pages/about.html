About us

History

The Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI) is a scientific society established in 1972. It is non-profit organization, registered under the Andhra Pradesh (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350 Fasli (Act I of 1350 F.) on 4th December, 1972. Its membership is open to all those scientists interested in promotion of Plant Protection and is dedicated for that cause.

As per the 1972 memorandum, it was decided that the permanent headquarters of PPAI would be Hyderabad. The Association started functioning from the location of Central Plant Protection Training Institute and set up its office in the premises of the institute. In 1998, the office set up was shifted to ICAR – National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources and since then has been continuing to operate from this institute till this day. In 2017, PPAI was permitted to construct its own building in an area located under the jurisdiction of Professor Jayashankar Telangana State Agricultural University, Hyderabad. All the set ups of the Association will be gradually shifted to the new building and PPAI new premises will be fully operational by January 2025.

Aims and Objectives of the Association

Since inception, the aims and objectives mentioned in PPAI memorandum have been based on a set of core underlying aspirations of advancing the cause of plant protection research and development, disseminate plant protection knowledge and maintain a consistent link among the plant protection workers belonging to diverse organizations in the country. These objectives have undergone revision and modifications to align with advances in research and development, technologies as well as infrastructure growth in areas related to plant protection. Following are the existing objectives of the Association:

  • To advance the cause of plant protection by encouraging and promoting plant protection research and education in the country.
  • To provide a forum for dissemination of information related to plant protection through a journal.
  • To bring about exchange of views and ideas on plant protection by organising lectures, discussion, seminars, symposia etc.
  • To maintain a link between plant protection workers in India.
  • To involve in any such activity which will be useful for the advancement and adoption of plant protection techniques developed by the scientific community.
