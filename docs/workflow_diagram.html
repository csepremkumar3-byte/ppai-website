Official Editorial Pipeline

Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP)

Complete End-to-End Workflow: Author Submission → Payment Verification → Chief Editor Assignment → Reviewer Workspace → Decision Release

• Operational Rule: Associate Editor is currently bypassed — All reviews and assignments route directly through Chief Editor Console.
1

Author Submission & Payment Details

Actor: Author

The author completes the manuscript submission form on the portal with full metadata and payment proof.

Uploaded Manuscript Data:
• Auto-Tracking ID (e.g. IJPP-2026-XXXX)
• Manuscript Title, Abstract & Keywords
• Subject Discipline (Entomology, Pathology, etc.)
• Primary Manuscript File (DOCX / PDF)
Payment Verification Details:
• Payment Transaction ID (Bank / UPI reference)
• Payment Screenshot (Attached PNG/JPG proof)
• Author Declaration & Conflict of Interest Check
Automated Triggers Fired Upon Submission:
Email 1 → Sent to Author (Acknowledgment)
Includes Tracking ID, Paper Title, Abstract, Payment Transaction ID, and confirmation that payment screenshot was logged.
Email 2 → Sent to Chief Editor (New Submission Alert)
Contains full submission alert, author details, payment transaction ID, payment screenshot link + Attached Manuscript File.
2

Chief Editor Console — Author-Centric Card View

Screen: /portal/admin/

Submissions populate into the Chief Editor Console structured per Author (rather than detached per-manuscript cards). All manuscripts and payment details from an author are grouped within their dedicated Author Card:

3

Chief Editor Reviewer Assignment & Window Time

Actor: Chief Editor

Chief Editor selects an expert reviewer and specifies the review window deadline (e.g. 14 days).

Trigger Email 3 → Reviewer Invitation (via Brevo)
Sends paper title, abstract, and review deadline window time with an interactive button to Accept or Decline Review Invitation.
Automated Timer & Follow-Up Reminder
If the reviewer does not acknowledge or accept within the specified window time, an automated reminder email is triggered automatically.
4

Reviewer & Associate Editor Credentials Provisioning

Actors: Chief Editor, Reviewer & Associate Editor

The Chief Editor creates and provisions portal login credentials directly inside the application (/portal/admin/) for both Reviewers and Associate Editors.

Trigger Email 4 → Login Credentials Email (via Brevo)
Chief Editor creates account for designated role (Associate Editor or Reviewer) → system delivers Username, Password, and Direct Portal Login Link to the recipient.
Role-Based Login Flow: Newly provisioned users visit /login/, select their respective role chip (Reviewer or Associate Editor) under the username & password fields, sign in, and land directly on their designated console (/portal/reviewer/ or /portal/editor/).
5

Reviewer Evaluation Workspace

Screen: /portal/reviewer/evaluate/

Reviewer clicks assignment card from "Your Assignments" and submits structured evaluation:

1. Rubric Evaluation
• Originality (1-5)
• Methodology (1-5)
• Clarity (1-5)
• Significance (1-5)
2. Overall Recommendation
• Accept Without Revision
• Minor Revision
• Major Revision
• Reject Manuscript
3. Dual Feedback Channels
• Comments for Author (Shared with author after approval)
• Confidential Remarks (Strictly for Chief Editor only)
6

Chief Editor Moderation & Author Release

Final Decision & Release

Upon reviewer submission, evaluation immediately routes into Chief Editor window for final moderation and release.

Trigger Email 5 → Sent to Chief Editor
Alerts Chief Editor: "Peer review submitted for [Tracking ID] by reviewer. Ready for editorial moderation."
Chief Editor Action in Application:
• Chief Editor inspects reviewer recommendation, author comments, and confidential remarks.
• Chief Editor clicks Approve & Release Comments to Author with final decision (Accept, Revisions, Reject).
Trigger Email 6 → Sent to Author
Author receives official decision letter and constructive peer-review comments. Author dashboard updates in real time.
