Plant Protection Association of India (PPAI)
Indian Journal of Plant Protection (IJPP) — Editorial Workflow
Executive Operational Specification: Submission → Screening → Peer Review → Decision Release
Target Pipeline: Author to Chief Editor Direct Flow
Document Type: System Architecture Specification
Associate Editor: Bypassed (Direct to Chief Editor)
Operational Rule: The Associate Editor tier is currently inactive. All screening, reviewer assignment, credentials provisioning, and moderation release actions are executed directly within the Chief Editor Console (/portal/admin/).
1 Author Manuscript Submission & Payment Upload
Actor: Author
The author submits the manuscript online through the portal form with full bibliographic metadata and mandatory payment details.
Manuscript Data:
  • Unique Tracking ID (e.g. IJPP-2026-XXXX)
  • Title, Structured Abstract & 5–8 Keywords
  • Subject Discipline (Entomology, Pathology, etc.)
  • Manuscript DOCX/PDF & Supplementary Data
Payment Information:
  • Payment Transaction ID (UPI / Bank UTR)
  • Payment Screenshot (Attached image proof)
  • Submission Declaration & Conflict of Interest
Automated Trigger 1 → Email to Author (Acknowledgment): Immediate delivery of Tracking ID, Paper Title, Abstract, Payment Transaction ID, and confirmation that payment screenshot proof was logged.
Automated Trigger 2 → Email to Chief Editor: Full submission alert with Tracking ID, author affiliation, payment transaction ID, payment screenshot link, and the attached manuscript file for direct screening.
2 Chief Editor Console — Author-Centric Card View (/portal/admin/)
Screen: Chief Editor Console
Submissions are populated into the Chief Editor Console structured per Author rather than detached per-manuscript cards. All submissions and payment proofs by a given author are organized within their dedicated Author Card.
Author-Centric Card Display Metrics (Card Set Per Author):
  • Author Identity & Profile: Corresponding Author Name, Registered Email, Institutional Affiliation, and Registration Date.
  • Grouped Author Manuscripts: All papers lodged by this author listed with Tracking ID, Full Title, Submission Date & Discipline.
  • Payment Verification per Paper: Direct display of Transaction ID (Bank/UPI) and one-click preview of Payment Screenshot proof.
  • Chief Editor Quick Actions: Download Full Manuscript, View Abstract, and Initiate Reviewer Assignment per submission.
3 Chief Editor Reviewer Assignment & Window Time
Actor: Chief Editor
After verifying payment and technical scope from the author's card, the Chief Editor assigns a specialized peer reviewer and defines an explicit evaluation window (e.g. 14 days).
Automated Trigger 3 → Reviewer Invitation Email (via Brevo): Sent immediately to reviewer containing paper title, abstract, and acceptance deadline window with interactive "Accept / Decline Review" buttons.
Automated Timer & Follow-up Reminder: If the reviewer has not acknowledged within the specified window time, an automated reminder email is dispatched to ensure timely turnaround.
4 Reviewer & Associate Editor Credentials Provisioning
Actors: Chief Editor, Reviewer & Associate Editor
The Chief Editor creates and provisions portal login credentials directly inside the Chief Editor Console (/portal/admin/) for both Reviewers and Associate Editors.
Automated Trigger 4 → Credentials Email (via Brevo): Chief Editor registers user with designated role (Associate Editor or Reviewer) → system dispatches Login Email, Initial Password, and Portal URL (/login/).
Role-Based Login Flow: Newly provisioned user visits /login/, selects their designated role chip (Reviewer or Associate Editor) under the username & password fields, signs in, and lands directly on their dedicated dashboard (/portal/reviewer/ or /portal/editor/).
5 Reviewer Evaluation Workspace (/portal/reviewer/evaluate/)
Screen: Reviewer Workspace
Reviewer clicks the manuscript card under "Your Assignments" to enter the peer-review workspace and submit comprehensive feedback.
1. Standardized Rubric & Decision:
  • Originality, Technical Quality, Clarity, Significance (Scores 1 to 5)
  • Overall Recommendation: Accept / Minor Revision / Major Revision / Reject
2. Dual Feedback Channels:
  • Comments for Author: Blinded constructive feedback (held until editor approval).
  • Confidential Remarks: Visible strictly to Chief Editor only.
6 Chief Editor Review Moderation & Author Release
Final Decision & Release
Upon submission by the reviewer, the evaluation flows immediately to the Chief Editor for quality control and final release.
Automated Trigger 5 → Alert to Chief Editor: Notifies Chief Editor that the review for [Tracking ID] is completed and ready for inspection.
Chief Editor Approval in Application:
  • Chief Editor reviews scores, author comments, and confidential remarks.
  • Chief Editor clicks Approve & Release Comments to Author and issues the formal editorial decision.
Automated Trigger 6 → Formal Decision Email to Author: Author receives official editorial letter with moderated review comments. Author portal dashboard updates status simultaneously.

(/portal/admin/)		 Chief Editor creates credentials for both Reviewer and Associate Editor. Users log in at /login/. • To User (via Brevo): Login Username, Password & Portal URL.
5. Evaluation Reviewer
(/portal/reviewer/evaluate/)		 Submits rubric scores, recommendation, Author Comments, and Confidential Editor Remarks. Review marked complete; author comments locked pending Chief Editor approval.

(/portal/admin/)		 Inspects confidential notes & scores. Approves comments and issues formal decision (Accept / Revision / Reject). • To Chief Editor: Review complete notice.
• To Author: Final decision letter with peer review comments.
